01 / The identity
01 · The identity

A folded ribbon,
resolved as a diamond.

RIVA's symbol is a single continuous ribbon folded into a diamond aperture. The wordmark is a geometric monoline sans, drawn as outlines — not set in a font. Both are fixed. Production may change scale, never shape.

Construction

The symbol is drawn on a 45° grid. Every outer edge resolves to 0° or 45°; collinear points are removed. The ribbon fold is negative space, so the mark holds in one colour.

Production note

The light reference used a secondary steel-blue on the ribbon. In single-colour production the ribbon is expressed as negative space — the same treatment already used by RIVA's dark lockup, favicon and app icon.

The wordmark is outlined. No font is required to open, render or print the logo.

03 · Proportion & space

Built once.
Scaled exactly.

Construction rules

  • Symbol edges resolve to 0° or 45°.
  • 11 anchor points; no collinear nodes.
  • Wordmark shares one cap line and baseline.
  • Stem widths are uniform; R bowl keeps its tangencies.
  • Scale the whole lockup by one factor — never re-space.
Do

Scale the whole lockup by one factor. Keep the clear space free of text, edges and other marks.

Do not

Re-space the symbol and wordmark, change the cap height, or let any element enter the clear-space zone.

04 / Application
04 · Application

The right variant,
on the right surface.

Light surfaces

Sidebar, top bar, documents, light landing pages. Use the blue lockup on Warm White or white.

Dark surfaces

Dark UI, hero sections, app splash, print on dark stock. Use the white lockup on Deep Navy.

App icon system

Actual pixel sizes. 128 / 256 / 512 / 1024 px are also supplied.

iOS

1024 × 1024 square PNG, opaque, no rounded corners. iOS applies the mask. A 180 px Apple Touch icon is included.

Android adaptive

Foreground and background are separate. The symbol sits inside the 66 dp safe zone on a 108 dp canvas.

PWA

192 and 512 px any-purpose icons, plus a 512 px maskable icon whose content stays inside the 40% safe radius.

Favicon

Dedicated 16 / 32 / 48 px artwork with a tighter mark. ICO and SVG are both supplied.

Do

Use the supplied square master and let the platform mask it. Keep the symbol optically centred.

Do not

Bake rounded corners into iOS artwork, stretch an icon to fill a slot, or place the full lockup inside an app icon.
